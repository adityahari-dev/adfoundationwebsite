    About AD Foundation

    Founded by Archit Deshpande, AD Foundation (Accelerated Development Foundation) was created to make high-quality tutoring accessible, affordable, and effective for every student.

    AD Foundation was created with a simple mission: to provide affordable, high-quality tutoring that helps students build confidence and achieve academic success.

    We believe every student deserves personalized support and access to educational opportunities that help them reach their full potential.

    Our tutors work closely with K–4 students to strengthen foundational skills, improve academic performance, and develop lifelong learning habits.
